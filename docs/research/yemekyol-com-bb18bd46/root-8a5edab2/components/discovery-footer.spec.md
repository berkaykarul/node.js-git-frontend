# NeighborhoodSection and SiteFooter Specification

## NeighborhoodSection
- White section, `64px 24px` padding, `64px 40px` at 768px, max-width 1180px.
- Heading 28px mobile / 32px medium, bold.
- Tab list starts 24px below heading, 24px gap, 1px `#e0e0e0` bottom border. Buttons are 16px with 12px bottom padding; selected is bold with 2px black bottom border, inactive is medium gray with transparent border.
- Grid starts 32px below tabs. Two columns by default, three at 640px, five at 768px; 32px horizontal and 16px vertical gaps.

## SiteFooter
- White footer, 24px horizontal, 32px top, 64px bottom; 40px horizontal at 768px; max-width 1180px.
- Popular Categories heading 20px bold; category links 14px gray in 2/3/4 column responsive grid.
- Detail columns begin after 48px margin, 1px top border, 40px top padding; one column below 768px and three above. Headings 16px bold; list begins 16px below and uses 8px row spacing.
- Legal row begins after 48px margin, 1px border, 32px top padding. It stacks on mobile and becomes a spaced row at 768px.
